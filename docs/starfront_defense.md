# Starfront Defense v1.2

New expeditions use the `game_mode` setting on `ExpeditionRules` (`defense` by default); select `exploration` for the previous rules. Version-1 saves remain in exploration mode. Version-2 defense saves migrate to version 3 with deterministic, inactive reinforcement identities based on the existing attacker profile; migration does not consume the saved gameplay RNG. Version-3 saves validate the fleet, reinforcement countdown, deferred activity time, navigation, battle marker and result ledger.

The player begins near a terrestrial homeworld with two incoming attackers. Movement advances strategic time in proportion to actual distance travelled. Completed PoI interactions add 1.5 strategic seconds and completed card battles add 2.5; opening or inspecting a site costs no time. Activity penalties advance attackers and reinforcement timers without starting a chained stationary battle; existing saves retain compatibility with deferred activity time. Two original attackers arriving without a neutralization end the expedition because the prescribed reveal condition would otherwise be unreachable.

Neutralizing an attacker by destruction or EMP reveals the stationary mothership. After its reveal, reinforcements begin after nine strategic seconds, then every twelve, with at most three active attackers and four reinforcements per system. A full fleet defers one spawn rather than accumulating a burst. EMP grants no destruction Salvage and uses the existing battle-result, progression and reward rules. Defeating or disabling the mothership secures remaining threats, stops reinforcement scheduling and unlocks voluntary FTL. Each jump generates a fresh frontline while retaining expedition-wide progression.

`ExpeditionState` owns progression, the result ledger and the current `DefenseState`. `NavigationState` owns player position and travel. The defense state receives both movement-derived and activity-derived seconds through one event-ordered simulation, owns the reinforcement countdown and unit identities, and computes swept contacts and analytic pursuit destinations. `SystemGenerator` creates encounter profiles and inactive reinforcement records deterministically. The 3D map renders snapshots, accepts input and displays bounded trails; it makes no strategic decisions. The save codec validates and restores authoritative snapshots without reporting movement.

Tune `resources/starfront_defense_tuning.tres` in the Inspector: player speed 7 units/s; attacker speed 1.2 units per strategic second; conversion 1/7 strategic seconds per unit travelled; interception radius 2.5; deployment distance 22; base integrity 100; arrival damage 20; mothership EMP bonus +2 charge; PoI cost 1.5 seconds; battle cost 2.5 seconds; first reinforcement 9 seconds; subsequent interval 12 seconds; active-attacker cap 3; reinforcement limit 4; and marker hold 0.4 seconds. The mothership has no strategic movement and its 2.5× visual scale does not change collision or combat statistics.

Hold an enemy or PoI marker to inspect its existing interface without navigation, camera focus or actionable controls. On touch devices, one tap selects and a second tap on the same target within 0.35 seconds starts travel; desktop clicks remain single-click travel. Dragging or pinching cancels the hold; a tap outside closes the preview. Trails are visual, bounded to 96 points per moving ship, and released when a hostile becomes inactive.

Defense systems have eight optional PoIs, omit unstable wormholes, and retain a procedurally styled sun, homeworld, additional planets and orbital rings. Cosmetic generation remains separate from gameplay RNG.

Defense enemy scaling uses the existing progression resource: previously all ranks gained 3 hull and 2 shields per difficulty step; standard enemies now start with 2 fewer shields and gain 2 hull and 1 shield per step, while elite and boss enemies start with 1 extra shield and gain 4 hull and 3 shields per step. Resource and production progression and EMP rules are unchanged. Exploration retains the previous values.

Future enemy variants can extend the existing unit records and profile selection in `SystemGenerator`. A future objective can replace `system_objective_complete()` and the focused reveal predicate. Celestial hazards can be added as explicit simulation events before presentation is updated. None are implemented in this pass.

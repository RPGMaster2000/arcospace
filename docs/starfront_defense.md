# Starfront Defense prototype

New expeditions use the `game_mode` setting on `ExpeditionRules` (`defense` by default); set it to `exploration` for the previous rules. Version-1 or older saves always resume as exploration and are never populated with invented defense units. Version-2 saves explicitly encode the selected mode and the validated defense snapshot. The existing one-slot checkpoint and unresolved-battle marker remain in use.

The player begins near a terrestrial homeworld with two incoming attackers. Travel distance is the sole source of strategic advancement. Two attacker arrivals without a neutralization end the expedition, since the prescribed reveal condition would otherwise become unreachable while the base still had 60 integrity. A neutralization by destruction or EMP reveals the mothership. EMP awards no destruction Salvage; the existing battle ledger, rewards and upgrade rules are reused. The mothership is stationary and has a flat additional EMP requirement. Once neutralized, remaining attackers are secured, optional sites remain accessible, and FTL opens a fresh defense system while preserving expedition progression.

`ExpeditionState` owns the active mode, progression, result ledger and a `DefenseState`. `NavigationState` owns player position and validated movement samples; the defense state consumes those samples and calculates simultaneous swept contacts and base arrivals. It owns unit identities, positions and status, base integrity, reveal readiness and threat completion. Units reference existing encounter indices. `SystemGenerator` deploys a seeded roster and site anchors, while the 3D map and its `defense_presentation.gd` adapter only display snapshots, exhaust and bounded trail histories. The coordinator gates movement sampling during modal screens and handles battle/transition presentation. The save codec validates and restores snapshots without reporting movement.

Tune `resources/starfront_defense_tuning.tres` in the Inspector: player speed 12 units/s, attacker speed 1.2 units per strategic second, conversion 1/12 strategic seconds per unit moved, interception radius 2.5 units, deployment radius 20 units, base integrity 100, arrival damage 20, and mothership EMP bonus +2 charge. The stationary mothership has no movement-speed tuning. Trail history uses at most 96 points per ship and fades while the player can plan or travel; visual history is not saved.

The three unit records in `DefenseState` are the natural place to extend concrete fleet behavior, with encounter profiles still selected by `SystemGenerator` and combat results still recorded by `ExpeditionState`. A future alternate objective can replace `system_objective_complete()` and the focused reveal predicate. Celestial hazards can be added as explicit navigation/simulation events before presentation is updated. These extensions are not implemented here.

# Salvage economy and recovery

Permanent field repairs cost **1 Salvage per Hull** and stop at the ship's
permanent Hull capacity. The star-map Hull panel shows the exact cost before
confirmation. Current battle results send players back to the map for repairs.
Trading Outpost Hull repairs still cost 1 per Hull and Ablative Plating costs
2 per point. The Kinetic Turret still costs 12 Salvage.

## Starfront Defense rewards

| Enemy | Hull destruction or Systems Failure | EMP recovery |
|---|---:|---:|
| Ordinary | Base +5 | 3 |
| Elite | Base after Elite multiplier +10 | 6 |
| Mothership or Boss rank | Base +20 | 10 |

The mothership also pays **10 Salvage once per secured system**. All three
victory methods secure it and unlock the existing FTL progression. The
encounter and completion claim are saved with the balance before the result
screen offers Continue. Older saves already marked secured keep their balance.
Defense EMP recovery replaces the combat payout and keeps the existing
no-upgrade rule. Exploration EMP escapes still award zero Salvage.

The Mining Operation's **Reactivate Extraction** approach spends 4 strategic
seconds before starting. It pays 3 Salvage at 15, 30, 45 and 60 committed
strategic seconds after activation. It resolves the PoI, which makes the
other four choices unavailable. Travel and committed activities advance the
clock. Menus and time spent playing a card battle leave it paused. The save
records the resolved source, amount paid and time to the next payout. Securing
the system, leaving it or ending the expedition stops extraction.

## Representative balances

Each paired value reads **before → after**. The examples use the Marauder's
10-Salvage reward, Marauder Vanguard's 20 × 2 Elite reward, the Sentinel
Dreadnought's 30-Salvage base reward and the 12-Salvage Kinetic Turret.
These are transaction calculations using the default rules.

| Scenario | Starting Salvage | Existing reward | New bonus or recovery | Repair expenditure | Turret expenditure | Final Salvage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Marauder destroyed, repair 5 Hull | 0 | 10 | 0 → 5 | 10 → 5 | 0 | 0 → 10 |
| Marauder destroyed, repair 10 Hull | 10 | 10 | 0 → 5 | 20 → 10 | 0 | 0 → 15 |
| Marauder Vanguard destroyed, repair 10 Hull | 0 | 40 | 0 → 10 | 20 → 10 | 0 | 20 → 40 |
| Marauder EMP, repair 5 Hull | 10 | 0 | 0 → 3 | 10 → 5 | 0 | 0 → 8 |
| Mothership EMP, system secured | 0 | 0 | 0 → 20 | 0 | 0 | 0 → 20 |
| Mothership destroyed, system secured | 0 | 30 | 0 → 30 | 0 | 0 | 30 → 60 |
| Two Marauders destroyed, repair 10 Hull, then try to buy a turret | 8 | 20 | 0 → 10 | 20 → 10 | 0 → 12 | 8 → 16 |
| Mining, compare Strip the Facility with Reactivate Extraction at 30 seconds | 0 | 12 → 0 | 0 → 6 | 0 | 0 | 12 → 6 |
| Same mining comparison at 60 seconds, if system remains contested | 0 | 12 → 0 | 0 → 12 | 0 | 0 | 12 → 12 |

The old turret scenario leaves only 8 Salvage after repairs, so the purchase
fails. The new balance covers it and leaves 16. Passive mining is a delayed
alternative to taking 12 upfront. It reaches the same maximum only if the
player commits 60 strategic seconds before securing or leaving the system.
